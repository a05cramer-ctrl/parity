FAQ

Straight answers.

How can it be below list price?

Parity buys capacity in volume and pays the saving through. Today's sample numbers show up to 6% under each lab's published price, platform fee included.

Do you swap my model for a cheaper one?

No. Requests go to the model you name and responses carry the provider's request ID.

Do you store prompts?

No. Traffic goes only to zero-retention endpoints.

Is $PARITY the same as credits?

No. Credits pay for usage and are not a token. $PARITY is the coin holders stake for credit rewards.

Is the gateway live?

Not yet. This page is a demonstration: prices, models and usage numbers are samples.
